02 — Your day

A plan built from what you got wrong.

Every day is four moves, in order. Nothing generic — each step is drawn from the questions you actually missed and the patterns hiding underneath them.

01
01 · CLEAR REVIEW

Clear yesterday's review

Start by closing the loop on the questions you missed last session, while the reasoning is still fresh.

02
02 · SPOTTED PATTERN

Work a spotted pattern

RampUp finds the thread across your misses — an amino-acid trap, a ratio you keep flipping — and gives you a focused set on exactly that.

03
03 · NEW GROUND

Learn something new

Then you move forward: fresh material at your current level for the subject, so you are always ramping, never idling.

04
04 · TEST CONDITIONS

Sit a timed exam

On exam days you sit a full timed block under real conditions — the only way to know a level has actually stuck.

03 — Rampy

Rampy explains the question you just missed.

Not a right-answer key. Rampy walks the whole question the way a good tutor would — why your choice was tempting, where the reasoning broke, and the move that gets you to the answer next time.

Bio / Biochem · Question 14
A non-competitive inhibitor is added to an enzyme assay. What happens to Vmax and Km?
BVmax unchanged, Km increases
CVmax decreases, Km unchanged
You picked the competitive pattern — that's the one where Km moves. Non-competitive inhibitors bind away from the active site, so no amount of substrate outcompetes them: Vmax drops, Km holds. Anchor it to the Lineweaver-Burk lines meeting on the x-axis.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered straight.

01What does "starts where you are" actually mean?

Each of the four AAMC sections has its own level, from Preview through full MCAT difficulty. You might sit at Hard in Bio/Biochem and Easy in CARS on the same day. RampUp ramps each one independently instead of pretending you're a single number.

02How is the daily plan generated?

From the questions you got wrong. Every day is four moves: clear yesterday's review, work a pattern spotted across your misses, learn new material at your current level, and — on exam days — sit a timed block. It's built from your history, not a fixed calendar.

03What's in the question bank?

5,000+ practice and exam questions across all four sections, each written to AAMC content categories and to test reasoning over recall, plus 7 timed exams including 2 full-length sims. Rampy walks through every question — all four choices, why the right one is right and the trap in each wrong one.

04Is there a free version?

No free-forever tier. Both plans include a 7-day free trial so you can run the full product — every level, every walkthrough, every exam — before you're charged. One subscription, everything included.

05What's the difference between the two plans?

Only the billing. The 3-month plan is $49.99 up front, which works out to $16.66/mo. The monthly plan is $19.99/mo, cancel anytime. Both unlock exactly the same product and both start with the 7-day free trial.

06How does the 511+ Guarantee work?

Answer at least 1,000 questions in RampUp, complete both full-length practice exams, then sit your official MCAT. If your score comes in below 511, email us your AAMC score report and we refund your subscription. It's a money-back promise, not a score prediction.

07Can I use RampUp on my phone?

Yes. RampUp: MCAT Head Start is on the App Store, with your levels, daily plan, walkthroughs, and timed exams all synced to your account.

08Is RampUp affiliated with the AAMC?

No. RampUp is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Association of American Medical Colleges. MCAT is a registered trademark of the AAMC.
