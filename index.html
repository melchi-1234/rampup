Bio / Biochem · Question 14

A non-competitive inhibitor is added to an enzyme assay. What happens to Vmax and Km?

B Vmax unchanged, Km increases
C Vmax decreases, Km unchanged
Walkthrough

You picked the competitive pattern — that's the one where Km moves. Non-competitive inhibitors bind away from the active site, so no amount of substrate outcompetes them: Vmax drops, Km holds. Anchor it to the Lineweaver-Burk lines meeting on the x-axis.
