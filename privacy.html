Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Short version: no tracking, no ads, and no account unless you want one. Your progress stays on your device by default. Two things reach the internet, and each only if you choose it: Rampy, the in-app tutor, when you tap it; and optional sync, if you sign in with your email to carry your progress between devices.

What we collect

By default, no personal information at all. RampUp works with no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not sell or share anything, and there are no advertising or analytics SDKs in RampUp.

The one exception is optional sync. If you choose to sign in, we receive your email address — so we can send your sign-in code and store your progress under your account. That is the only personal information RampUp ever collects, and only if you turn sync on. We never receive your name, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising identifier, because RampUp never asks for them.

What stays on your device

Everything, except the Rampy requests and — if you turn it on — optional sync, both described below. Specifically: all practice questions, all exam questions, every concept lesson and every narrated explanation are stored in the app itself. Your answers, progress, streak, daily goal, review schedule, bookmarks, exam scores, your starting levels and your daily plan are kept in local storage on the device you are using, are not transmitted unless you turn on sync, and are erased if you clear your browser data, delete the app, or use “Reset everything” in Settings.

The same is true of the onboarding on this website. Your coursework answers and the levels calculated from them are stored in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Progress sync (optional)

Sync is off unless you turn it on. If you sign in with your email in Settings, two things are then stored on our side: your email address, and your study progress (the same stats, streak, review pile and bookmarks described above). They are kept in our database — Supabase, hosted in the United States — so your other devices can load them when you sign in there too.

Your sign-in codes are delivered by email through Resend. Signing out on a device stops that device syncing and leaves your local progress untouched. Deleting your account removes the synced copy from our database. Sync changes nothing else: no tracking, no ads, and we never sell your data. Your questions to Rampy and your reading passages are never part of sync.

Rampy, and the one time RampUp uses the internet

Rampy is the tutor built into RampUp. Most of what it does happens entirely on your device with no network connection at all — explaining why an answer is right, why the one you picked is wrong, and finding another question on the same concept. Those never leave your device.

Some of what Rampy can do needs a language model, which cannot run on your device. If, and only if, you tap one of those options, RampUp sends a single request to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's API and returns the answer. Nothing is sent unless you tap.

What that request contains: the practice question you are looking at, its answer choices, which one you picked, the correct answer, and RampUp's own written explanation and per-choice notes — so the tutor answers from our material rather than improvising. It also includes the subject, the difficulty level you are working at, how many questions you have answered in that subject and what percentage you got right, and how many days until your test, so the answer can be pitched at the right level.

What that request deliberately does not contain: your name, your email, or any account identifier — the tutor request carries none of them, even if you have signed in to sync. Which courses you told us you have taken. And reading passages are never sent at all — CARS passages and science passages stay on your device.

The one identifier: a random string generated on your device the first time you open RampUp, sent with these requests so we can stop any single device from running up an unreasonable number of them. It is not linked to you, it is not linked to anything else, and it cannot be traced back to a person. Erasing the app erases it.

What our server records: that a request happened — the random string, which kind of request it was, how long it took and how large it was. Not the question, and not the answer Rampy gave. Rampy conversations are not stored anywhere, including on your device; the answer disappears when you close it.

Payments

We never see or store your card details.

On iPhone and iPad, purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. RampUp asks Apple what you are entitled to; that check carries no personal information and none of your practice data. We do not receive your name, email or Apple Account from Apple.

On the web, payment is handled by Stripe, who collect the details needed to take payment and are the controller of that information under their own privacy policy. After you pay, RampUp sends only the checkout session ID from your return link to our verification endpoint, which asks Stripe whether that session was actually paid. We store nothing from that exchange except the unlock, on your device.

Third parties in the path

  • Cloudflare runs our two small server functions and, like any host, sees the request including the originating IP address.
  • Anthropic receives the Rampy prompt and returns the answer.
  • Stripe processes web payments.
  • Supabase stores your account and your synced progress, and only if you turn on sync. It is our database and authentication provider, hosted in the United States.
  • Resend delivers your sign-in code emails, and only if you turn on sync.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website and, like any web host, records ordinary server logs. We use those only as aggregate traffic counts and they are not linked to anything you do in the app.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children

RampUp is study material for a graduate-school admissions test and is not directed at children. Aside from optional sync, it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

If you have not turned on sync, we hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. You control your own data directly: “Reset everything” in Settings erases your progress, and clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes it entirely. If you want a copy of what is stored, it is all in your device's local storage.

If you have turned on sync, your email address and your synced progress are held under your account. Signing out stops syncing and leaves your device untouched. To remove the stored copy entirely, email us at the address below and we will delete your account and its synced row.
