Build the risky part first.

The order I wish I had before I made two apps: one MCAT product and one boba focus app. This is not a stack shopping list. It is the sequence from idea to something another person can actually trust.

A prototype proves one dangerous assumption. A production product handles the boring paths after the demo works.

Write the painful moment, not the feature list

“I touch one notification and lose the session” is buildable. “An all-in-one productivity platform” is not.

Output: one sentence and one person you can show it to.

Choose the riskiest behavior

For Mr. Tapioca it was real iPhone app blocking. For RampUp it was whether a learner could move from a question into a useful next step.

Output: one behavior that can visibly pass or fail.

Make the ugliest working prototype

Use the tools you can operate. Hard-code the happy path if you must, but label it a prototype and do not confuse a scripted demo with a product.

Output: a screen recording of the risky behavior working.

Put it in front of the exact person

Do not ask “would you use this?” Give them the task. Watch where they stop, hesitate, or reach for an explanation.

Output: observed behavior and the first repeated failure.

Build the failure paths

Accounts, loading, no signal, retries, restoring purchases, expired links, duplicate actions, and recovery are where a demo starts becoming real.

Output: a checklist you can break on purpose.

Add trust before growth

Privacy, truthful claims, pricing, support, permissions, review rules, and measurement have to match what the product actually does.

Output: a stranger can understand the trade without knowing you.

Ship one measurable path

Give each post its own tracked destination. Change one important variable at a time. A click is not an install, and an install is not a retained user.

Output: one source tag and one result you can define honestly.

Before you call it production

The main behavior worksOn the real device or environment, not only in a mock.
Failure is recoverableA timeout or duplicate tap does not corrupt someone’s state.
Claims match evidenceNo invented users, outcomes, revenue, or readiness.
Someone else can use itWithout you narrating every click beside them.
See one of the real products

I am still learning this in public. This is the order that would have saved me time, not a promise that your app will work or make money.
